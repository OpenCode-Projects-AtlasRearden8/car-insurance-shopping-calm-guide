How this site works

  • No build step. Serve the site/ folder with any static server.
  • No network calls. Nothing is fetched, posted, or tracked.
  • No accounts. The worksheet saves into your browser's localStorage on this device only.
  • No personal data needed. The worksheet fields ask for carriers and premium numbers — never your license number or SSN.

Quick loop

  1. Open the worksheet, write your frozen coverage version.
  2. Collect three quotes (one call, one direct site, one independent agent).
  3. Log each as it arrives; the sheet normalizes terms to annual cost.
  4. Check the comparison page; verify the winner's fine print before paying anyone.
  5. Never cancel the old policy before the new one binds.
