Audit prompts

  • Does the cheapest quote have the same limits you froze? If not, it isn't cheaper — it's thinner.
  • Did it include rental and roadside? Price those separately if not.
  • 6-month or 12-month rate? Check the annual column, not the monthly.
  • Any discount promised on the phone shown on the written quote?
  • Will the price change at bind when credit/CLUE is checked? Ask (docs/04, rule 6).
