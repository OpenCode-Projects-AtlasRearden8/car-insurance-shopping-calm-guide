# 00 — Start Here

Welcome. If the words "comprehensive deductible" make your shoulders climb toward
your ears, this guide is for you. We are going to walk through shopping car
insurance the way a calm friend would: one small step at a time, no condescension,
no panic.

## What this guide is (and isn't)

**It is:** a plain-English walkthrough of what the coverage words mean, how to
choose sensible limits, how to gather and compare three quotes fairly, and how
to use AI chat as a homework helper without ever handing over your real
personal data.

**It isn't:** legal, financial, or insurance advice. Rules vary by state and by
policy. When the numbers get real, confirm with your state insurance department
and a licensed agent.

## The 20-minute version

1. **Learn the seven core coverage words** (chapter 01). That's the vocabulary test.
2. **Check your state minimums** (chapter 02), then decide whether "minimum" is
   actually sensible for you.
3. **Collect discounts** (chapter 03) — including the ones nobody mentions.
4. **Get three quotes and compare them apples-to-apples** (chapter 04) using the
   worksheet in `site/worksheet.html`.
5. **Decide honestly about full coverage** (chapter 05).
6. **Use AI as a homework helper, never a data source** (chapter 06).
7. **Before you call, run the checklist and keep your card small** (chapter 07).

## The one rule that keeps this calm

**You are gathering information, not committing.** A quote has no obligation
attached. Your current policy will not punish you for shopping. The conversational
phrases in chapter 07 (like "I'm comparing rates") are there because the right
words keep phone calls short and pressure low.

## Low-energy mode (ADHD-friendly)

Only have ten minutes? Do this:

1. Open `site/worksheet.html` in your browser.
2. Fill in what you know: vehicle, annual miles, coverage versions you want priced.
3. Get one quote online, save it, close the tab. Nothing is saved anywhere but
   your own browser.
4. Come back tomorrow. Two is better than one; three is the goal.
